How it works

NBA Edge puts the information sharp bettors look at in one place: travel, rest, referee crews, shooting form and who's missing, for every game, in plain English.

The totals lean

Four signals, each tested against 11 seasons of closing lines (2015-16 to 2025-26):

  • Whistle-heavy referee crew points to the over.
  • Lopsided spread (12+ points) points to the over.
  • Close spread (2 points or less) points to the under.
  • Both teams hot from three over their last 3 games points to the under.

Over signals minus under signals gives the lean. Historically, playing the lean won about 54% of the time, and it went the right direction in all 11 seasons. Break-even at standard odds is 52.4%. Past results don't guarantee future ones, which is why every lean this season is locked at tip-off and graded in public.

Next Man Up

When rotation players are out, we estimate which teammates absorb their minutes and compare the backups' combined rating with the missing players'. A score of 100 means the backups are as productive per minute.

Fatigue & travel

Rest days, back-to-backs, games in the last week, and the time zones and miles each team has covered in the last 10 days. Sportsbooks already price most of this into the line; it helps explain why the line is where it is.
